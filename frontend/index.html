✦ How Cureo Works

Your symptoms pass through 12 specialized AI agents in sequence

1
🔍 Triage Agent — Instantly checks for life-threatening emergencies. Triggers SOS alert if needed.
2
🩺 Symptom Analyzer — Extracts all symptoms and maps them to body parts for the 🫀 Body tab.
3
🧬 Diagnosis Agent — Suggests possible conditions with High / Medium / Low likelihood for 📊 Confidence bars.
4
💊 Treatment Agent — Recommends safe home care steps and warning signs to watch for.
5
🏥 Referral Agent — Decides which specialist to visit and how urgently.
6
🗺️ Heatmap Agent — Assigns pain intensity 1–10 to each body part, shown as heat colors on the body map.
7
❤️ Health Score Agent — Calculates a 0–100 wellness score shown as a gauge in the ❤️ Score tab.
8
🕸️ Symptom Web Agent — Maps connections between symptoms and conditions (used internally for context).
9
🌡️ Vitals Agent — Extracts temperature, heart rate, BP, O₂ and flags Normal / High / Low in the 🌡️ Vitals tab.
10
📡 Radar Agent — Scores 5 risk dimensions: Severity, Duration, Symptom Count, Emergency Risk, Age Risk.
11
🔬 Comparison Agent — Side-by-side comparison of top conditions with matching vs non-matching symptoms.
12
📋 Report Agent — Assembles all outputs into the final structured response shown in chat.

All 12 agents run in sequence in ~10 seconds · Powered by Mistral AI
